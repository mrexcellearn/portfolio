## Business case

Treasury handles about 130 payments a month that fall outside the normal schedule, such as urgent payments, import duty and employee reimbursements. They come from teams across different islands and countries.

Requests arrived <Pain>on paper, by email or verbally</Pain>. Approvals and filing were <Pain>fully paper-based</Pain>, and the <Pain>payment proof was sent by hand</Pain>.

## The build

### The app

<ToolPills tools={['Power Apps', 'SharePoint']} />

A canvas app with one home screen for everyone. Requesters create and track their requests. Finance works the queue from the admin screen, where every request carries its documents, its history and the next step. Once paid, the payment proof goes to the beneficiary by email.

### The approval flow

<ToolPills tools={['Power Automate', 'SharePoint', 'Teams Approvals', 'Outlook']} />

## Results

About 130 non-scheduled payments a month now come through <Win>one front door</Win>, and processing time per request is <Win>down 70%</Win>. Because every request is in the queue, <Win>no payment is skipped</Win> because of a lost document or a forgotten message.

Payment vouchers are <Win>fully digital</Win> and approved in Teams. Requesters follow their own payments and download the proof themselves, and every request keeps its documents, approvals and payment proof together, so <Win>the audit file is complete</Win> without a filing cabinet.
