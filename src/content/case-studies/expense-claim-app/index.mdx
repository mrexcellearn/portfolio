## Business case

About 60 expense claims a month <Pain>ran on paper</Pain>. Employees kept every receipt, taped them to a sheet, <Pain>typed a recap table by hand</Pain>, printed it and walked it to their manager for a signature.

Many managers sat <Pain>on another island or in another country</Pain>. Then the claim had to be scanned, emailed and e-signed. Once signed, it went to finance for review and posting, often <Pain>by courier</Pain> because finance was in a different office.

Every step cost paper, shipping and days. <Pain>Receipts went missing in transit</Pain>, and nobody could see where a claim was without asking.

### The approval flow

<ToolPills tools={['Power Automate', 'SharePoint', 'Teams Approvals', 'Outlook']} />

## Results

Claims are now <Win>fully digital</Win>. There are <Win>no printed recaps, no scanned signatures and no courier bills</Win>. Approvals happen in Teams, wherever the manager is, and finance reviews first, so problems are caught before a manager spends time on them.

About 40 employees submit their claims in the app. Every claim keeps its receipts, comments and approval history in one place, so <Win>the audit trail is complete</Win> without a filing cabinet.

## Next improvements

- Store the approval chain in a table instead of inside the flow, so a new approver doesn't need a flow change.
- Use AI Builder receipt processing to read the amount and date from the photo, instead of typing them.
- Add indexed columns from day one, because the lists grow every month.
