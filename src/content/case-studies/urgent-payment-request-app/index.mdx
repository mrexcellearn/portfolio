## Business case

Urgent payments <Pain>arrived by email</Pain>. A requester would forward an invoice, sometimes with the approval attached and sometimes without, and ask for the payment to go out the same day.

AP then re-checked every field by hand, posted an advance in SAP, built a payment voucher in Excel, and chased treasury for review. <Pain>Nothing showed the status of a request</Pain>, so requesters followed up by email too.

<Pain>Mistakes surfaced late.</Pain> A missing approval was often found only at treasury review, after AP had already done the posting and the voucher.

### The app

Four screens replace the email thread. Requesters can't submit without the documents, everyone sees the status, and AP and treasury work from the same record. Click the numbered pins to see how each part works.

### The cloud flow

A single flow runs on every new request. It returns incomplete requests straight away, and otherwise routes the request to treasury for approval and writes the outcome back to the list.

## Results

Requests now <Win>arrive complete</Win>, or they go straight back to the requester with a reason. AP spends its time on verification and posting instead of chasing attachments, and <Win>every request has a status anyone can check</Win>.
